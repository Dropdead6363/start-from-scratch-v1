Letter from Grandfather

You probably weren’t expecting this place, and I wasn’t expecting to leave it to you.

I bought these two acres intending to clean them up, fix the cabin, and build the farm out from here. I never got around to most of it.

You carry my name. That is about the only real connection I have left to this family, and I never knew you well enough to have bad memories of you. I know the others well enough that I don’t want them benefiting from something I bought.

The property, the truck, the buildings, and whatever is on the land are yours. There are no regular utility bills right now. You will still have the normal property costs and taxes.

Before I died, I set up local store advertisements to come to this mailbox: the hunting and outdoor store, hardware store, grocery, feed store, equipment dealer, and nursery. Use them or don’t.

I’m dead. What you do with the place is your business.
